# Practical Alternatives — Momentum Products in India

Status: research-context comparison only. This document does not alter the frozen primary strategy.

## Why this comparison exists

A retail research project should answer not only whether the frozen Top-5 strategy works versus NIFTY 50 TRI, but whether implementing it provides a meaningful benefit over an already available passive momentum product.

This is a **practical-value question**, not a replacement-strategy experiment.

## Current evidence checked 2026-10-07

### NIFTY200 Momentum 30

NSE's official index page describes NIFTY200 Momentum 30 as a 30-stock momentum index drawn from NIFTY 200. Its momentum score uses 6-month and 12-month price returns adjusted for volatility. The methodology uses factor-tilted weights, caps individual weights, and applies a rank buffer to reduce turnover.

Official sources:
- https://www.niftyindices.com/indices/equity/strategy-indices/nifty200-momentum-30
- https://www.niftyindices.com/indices/equity/strategy-indices/nifty-200-momentum-30-index-static

## Interpretation

These products do **not** invalidate the project because they are not the same strategy.

The project studies:

- PIT NIFTY 50;
- 12M formation;
- 1M skip;
- Top-5;
- quarterly rebalance;
- equal-target whole-share retail implementation;
- ₹25,000 primary capital.

NIFTY200 Momentum 30 instead uses a broader universe, 6/12-month volatility-adjusted momentum, 30 constituents, factor-tilted weighting and a turnover-reduction buffer.

Therefore the correct research question is:

> Does the much simpler, concentrated NIFTY 50 Top-5 implementation provide evidence of a sufficiently distinct or useful return/risk/implementation profile to justify implementing it directly rather than buying a low-cost passive momentum product?

This comparison must be reported after the frozen strategy evaluation and must not be used to select a replacement strategy after observing results.
